My Personal Interests

Ever since I got my smartphone back in 2020, I have rooted the phone and been working on blocking ads from the phone. I've always been annoyed with ads and gotten ad blockers on my computer, so I figured the phone wouldn't be too difficult. Boy, was I wrong, as Google works to prevent users from editing the hosts file in the phone's root directory to prevent this sort of thing.

My SAAB has been an interest of mine since my uncle had one back in 2010. This car has been the bane of my existence for the past few years as more and more expensive and time-consuming things keep needing work done. The car is not functional right now, but will be soon.

I have been collecting, or hoarding, old hardware for a while now. I have this old Dell PowerVault that allegedly came from Blizzard Entertainment back in the mid-2000s. I have yet to get it up and running, but recently picked up a good monitor to pair with the PowerVault.
